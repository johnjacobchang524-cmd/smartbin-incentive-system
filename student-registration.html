Research Consent Form

Please read the information below before registering for the SmartBin research project.

Research Title

Development of a Semi-Automated RFID-Enabled Smart Trash Bin for Promoting Proper Plastic and Paper Waste Disposal with an Incentive Points System.

Purpose of the Study

This study evaluates a SmartBin system designed to encourage proper plastic and paper waste disposal through an incentive points mechanism.

Information Collected

The system may collect your full name, Gmail address, student ID, disposal records, and incentive points.

Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary. Choosing not to participate will not affect your grades or your relationship with your classmates.

Privacy and Confidentiality

The researchers will take reasonable measures to protect your personal information and use it for the purposes explained in this consent form.

Questions and Concerns

You may ask the research team or research adviser questions before deciding whether to participate.
